I care about the people using what we build. A lot of my work happens behind the scenes, making it easier for teams to understand, deploy, and maintain their systems so they can focus on those users.

At <a href="https://aspiraconnect.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Aspira</a>, I own my team's delivery pipeline, from infrastructure as code and CI/CD to monitoring and security. I work with IT Ops and Security on policy, and mentor product engineers on cloud and deployment. I started in software as an app developer, so I still jump into app code when I find a bug or someone needs a hand. I also built an AI agent that helps engineers figure out why a pipeline build failed. Most of my work is in TypeScript on AWS.

At a prior job, Ensomata, I managed a backend and cloud infrastructure team of five engineers, ranging from junior to principal. We built systems to help medical assistants and physicians track and recommend preventative care for their patients. I also led our move to a monorepo and GitHub Actions, which made sharing code and deploying simpler.

As a manager, I cared about helping engineers grow and using everyone's strengths to make the team and product better. I wanted people to feel comfortable asking questions in public, sharing what they knew, and hopping into a Slack huddle to work through a problem together. We didn't need a hero to save the day. We lifted each other up, so no one was ever on their own. If something wasn't working, we'd talk about it.

I don't believe in meetings just to have meetings. We had clear agendas and used that time to solve problems or get on the same page. I enjoyed managing the team, and I'd do it again.

Before software engineering, I was a musician and an elementary teacher, and I worked in tech support. Those jobs gave me a lot of practice explaining things and helping people get unstuck.

I'm an <a href="https://builder.aws.com/community/heroes/DanielleHeberling" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">AWS Serverless Hero</a> and co-organize the <a href="https://www.believeinserverless.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Believe in Serverless Discord community</a>. I share what I'm learning, including the mistakes, in the hope that it makes things easier for someone else.
